Know Your Legal Entitlements

These rights are enforceable by statutory mandates under Indian Law. Police, courts, and welfare officers cannot decline these reliefs under any circumstance.

Regardless of your personal income or marital status, any female citizen filing under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act (PWDVA) or Indian Penal / BNSS laws has the absolute statutory right to a free, state-sponsored lawyer who will prepare petitions, appear in court, and secure injunctions.

task_alt No paperwork fees, court stamp charges, or process fees will be charged to you.

You cannot be dispossessed or evicted from your shared household without due legal procedure. Magistrates can prohibit respondents from entering your residence, workplace, or contacting your family, and can order alternate safe housing funded directly by the respondent.

task_alt Protection Officers ensure keys and belongings remain exclusively accessible to you.

The court is empowered to grant interim monetary relief for loss of earnings, medical expenses, and daily maintenance before final trials occur. Emergency disbursements can be triggered through the District Legal Services Authority within 48 hours in critical scenarios.

task_alt Direct bank transfers are shielded with statutory non-disclosure protocols.

All hospitals (public, private, or military) are legally mandated to furnish immediate emergency medical care, physical examination, forensic documentation, and trauma medication free of charge without waiting for formal police FIR lodgment.
