📌 1. Project Overview & Problem Addressed

Victims and witnesses under the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989, registered through NHAA 14566 or the Integrated Portal, face prolonged psychological distress after complaint registration. Threats, bail of accused, judicial delays, social ostracism, and compensation uncertainty compound their trauma across every stage of the legal process.

⚠️ The Gap Existing government mechanisms focus on legal and financial support. None provide continuous, proactive psychological monitoring of victim well-being throughout the investigation → trial → rehabilitation → compensation cycle.

Nyaya Sakhi fills this gap with a proactive, AI-driven distress prediction system that:

  • Continuously interacts with victims across chatbot, IVRS, SMS, WhatsApp, and Web Portal channels
  • Fuses voice prosody, text sentiment, behavioral signals, and legal case context into a single Dynamic Distress Score
  • Predicts deterioration before a crisis emerges and automatically routes interventions
  • Provides dashboards at district, state, and national levels for evidence-based oversight

Owning Ministry & Deployment Authority

✅ Single Ministry Ownership Nyaya Sakhi is designed exclusively for the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE) through the National Helpline Against Atrocities (NHAA 14566) and operates under the mandate of the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989. It has no dependency on or overlap with the Ministry of Women & Child Development, Mission Shakti, or One Stop Centres.

🔒 6. Security, Privacy & DPDP Act 2023

Encryption & Storage

  • AES-GCM-256 field-level encryption on all victim messages, phone numbers, and PII before disk write (privacy.py)
  • SQLite with encrypted sensitive fields — no plaintext PII at rest
  • Automated 730-day data retention policy

Access Control

  • Officer-gated purge endpoint (POST /api/officer/purge) via hmac.compare_digest constant-time comparison
  • OTP rate-limited to 3 attempts/hour per email
  • Victim Portal uses allowlist response model — raw risk scores never exposed

Consent Architecture

Every channel records consent_flag and consent_timestamp. If consent is withheld, standard data processing is restricted. A life-safety override applies only for P1-CRITICAL and P0-EMERGENCY tiers.

❓ 9. Judge Q&A — Anticipated Questions & Accurate Answers
1
Which ministry owns this system, and why is it MoSJE and not the Ministry of Women & Child Development?

Built exclusively for the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE) under the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989. Beneficiaries are victims registered through NHAA 14566 — the national helpline managed by MoSJE specifically for SC/ST atrocity cases.

The Ministry of Women & Child Development operates Mission Shakti and One Stop Centres — a separate mandate. There is no functional overlap. Nyaya Sakhi does not reference or deploy through WCD infrastructure.

2
What exactly does your AI analyse in voice messages? Do you do facial expression analysis?

No facial expression analysis. The system analyses acoustic prosody from voice recordings — measurable properties of the audio waveform:

  • Pitch variance (Hz): Below 10 Hz → flat, withdrawn affect; above 55 Hz with high vocal energy → agitation or panic.
  • Pause ratio (%): Above 35% → psychomotor slowing associated with severe distress.
  • Speaking rate (WPM): Below 90 WPM alongside high pause ratio → psychomotor slowing.

Extracted from Whisper ASR followed by Wav2Vec2 acoustic feature analysis — all from audio, not video. See speech_agent.py:28–47.

3
Is your 0–100% Distress Score clinically validated? Can it be used as a psychiatric diagnosis?

No, and we are explicit about this. The Dynamic Distress Score is an AI-derived triage indicator — not a clinical diagnosis. It answers: "Who needs a counsellor's attention most urgently right now?"

Not a DSM-5 severity rating or PHQ-9 equivalent. Every alert includes mandatory human_in_the_loop_status: "Awaiting Counselor Review". The AI surfaces priority; the licensed counsellor makes all clinical decisions.

4
Why does this need AI? Why can't a normal case management portal do the same thing?

A traditional portal stores and displays case data. It cannot:

  • Detect change over time — Behavioral Agent tracks response latency drift (usually 2h, now 24h) and consecutive missed check-ins.
  • Interpret language — NLP on 24 Indian languages to detect hopelessness, suicidal ideation, or intimidation from natural conversation text.
  • Read vocal signals — pitch flatness and speaking rate slowdown are invisible to a case manager reviewing a text record.
  • Synthesise all signals simultaneously — the synergy multiplier (×1.35 when bail granted + disengagement + high NLP distress co-occur) is a pattern no human can monitor at scale.
  • Act proactively — automatically dispatch a Twilio IVR emergency call without waiting for the victim to reach out.
5
How does your system handle a victim who says "I am fine" but is actually in distress?

Three complementary safeguards:

  1. Sentiment Guardrail (nlp_agent.py:29–45): Suppresses false positives for genuinely positive text, but does not suppress when crisis-pattern words co-occur. "I am fine but the accused threatened me" still triggers threat_violence_flag = True.
  2. Voice Prosody Override: Flat pitch (<10 Hz) or prolonged pauses (>35%) in the voice note contribute a speech distress score independently of the text content.
  3. Behavioral Context: 3 missed check-ins + message at 3 AM = high anomaly score regardless of what the text says.
6
Does your system support Hindi and regional languages?

Yes. The NLP model is fine-tuned IndicBERTv2 (Robbiinn/nyaya-sakhi-crisis-indicbert), which natively supports 24 Indian languages through multilingual pre-training. The model produces distress scores directly from Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, and other regional language text.

The Groq LLM counselor companion uses multilingual system prompts and responds in Hindi or English based on victim preference. IVRS scripts support regional TTS delivery via Twilio.

7
What happens when the AI model inference service is offline?

Explicit offline fallback in nlp_agent.py:109–138. When the IndicBERT API is unreachable:

  • Falls back to a regex-based crisis pattern scanner (kill, suicide, threat, rape, danger, harm, attack).
  • If crisis patterns detected: distress_score = 0.95, all critical flags set.
  • If no crisis patterns: distress_score = 0.05 and analysis_source = "indicbertv2_offline_standby" recorded — counsellor dashboard shows fallback mode indicator.

The system always provides a triage signal, even without the ML model.

8
How do you protect victim privacy? What if data is leaked?
  • AES-GCM-256 encryption on all sensitive fields before writing to the database — privacy.py.
  • Consent architecture: every channel records consent_flag and consent_timestamp.
  • PII masking in NLP pipeline: clean_text() masks phone numbers ([PHONE]) and emails ([EMAIL]) before sending to the ML model.
  • Officer-gated purge: POST /api/officer/purge uses hmac.compare_digest constant-time comparison.
  • Victim Portal allowlist: raw distress scores are never exposed to the victim-facing client.
  • 730-day automated retention: compliant with DPDP Act 2023.
9
What if the AI gives a wrong prediction?
  • Sentiment Guardrail: Genuine positive messages suppress false distress scoring before reaching the counsellor queue.
  • XAI Explanation: Every alert includes a human-readable clinical_reasons list — counsellor sees exactly why the score was elevated and can rapidly dismiss if reasons are not compelling.
  • Risk Tiers as Filter: Only OUTREACH (≥0.50) and URGENT (≥0.75) trigger active counsellor notification. ROUTINE and WATCH are passive only.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Mandatory: The system cannot take clinical action autonomously.
10
Does your system cover victims through the entire judicial process?

Yes. context_agent.py reads these lifecycle fields for every victim interaction:

  • case_stage: Trial / Cross-Examination stages add +0.20 to context risk weight.
  • accused_bail_status: "Granted" adds +0.45 — the highest safety vulnerability penalty.
  • threat_reported: Adds +0.50 when active threat is logged.
  • hearing_postponed: Institutional delay adds +0.15.
  • compensation_status: "Rejected" adds +0.20 — models financial and psychological distress of a denied claim.

All five fields are first-class columns in the victims database table.

11
What is the difference between the Groq LLM (Track 1) and the LangGraph agents (Track 2)?
  • Track 1 — Groq LLM: Victim-facing conversational response — warm, empathetic, multilingual text in ~400ms. Provides emotional support, surfaces NHAA 14566 helpline. Does not make risk assessments.
  • Track 2 — LangGraph Multi-Agent Pipeline: Runs silently in the background. 4 specialist agents analyse the message, outputs are fused into a risk score, XAI explanation generated, escalation agent decides on alerts.

The victim always receives a human-feeling response (Track 1) while the system simultaneously makes a risk decision (Track 2) without introducing clinical language into the victim-facing interaction.

12
How do you ensure victims are not re-traumatised by the chatbot?

The Groq LLM counselor persona (counselor_persona.py) operates under hard-coded non-clinical guardrails: never identifies itself as a doctor; never predicts court outcomes or bail decisions; always surfaces NHAA 14566 helpline and emergency number 112; responses are warm, validating, and non-alarmist. Verified by 39+ automated tests in test_counselor_persona.py.

13
What are the innovation components — what is genuinely new here?
  1. Multi-signal temporal fusion: No existing government victim support system fuses text sentiment + voice prosody + engagement velocity + live legal case context into a single calibrated risk score.
  2. Synergy Multiplier: Detection of concurrent risk co-occurrence (bail granted + disengagement + high NLP distress simultaneously) — not present in standalone NLP or voice systems.
  3. Proactive omnichannel check-ins: The system contacts victims — they do not need to initiate. Most AI helplines are purely reactive.
  4. Longitudinal distress tracking: Score evolves over time; a rising trend is detected even if each individual score is moderate.
  5. XAI-first architecture: Explainability built in from day one — every alert includes a clinical_reasons list built from each agent's contribution.

15
What is the SOS panic button and is it different from AI-detected escalation?
  • AI-Detected Escalation: LangGraph pipeline infers risk from language, voice, and behaviour — runs on every inbound message automatically.
  • Manual SOS Panic Button: Victim-initiated from web portal or Telegram. Skips all NLP scoring — goes directly to P0-EMERGENCY priority. Triggers Twilio Voice calls to pre-configured SOS recipients. 5-minute deduplication window. Logged as manual_sos trigger type.
16
How does the system handle a victim who stops responding entirely?
  • 7+ days since last check-in: Adds +0.35 to behavioral anomaly score, sets disengagement_flag = True.
  • 3+ consecutive missed check-ins: Adds +0.50 — alone can push the score into the OUTREACH tier.
  • Synergy with legal context: 7+ days silence + bail just granted → synergy multiplier (×1.35) → likely URGENT tier → automated IVRS call triggered.

The most at-risk victims are often the least likely to call. Nyaya Sakhi detects the absence of engagement as a distress signal — the core capability most helplines lack.

17
What interventions does the system recommend, and does it execute them?

Context-aware recommendations:

  • High fear/grief + bail granted → Counselling session + witness protection alert
  • Voice tremor + night messaging → Urgent counsellor callback + medical referral
  • Compensation status "Rejected" → Legal aid assignment + financial assistance scheme
  • Suicidal ideation keywords → Immediate IVRS emergency call + 24×7 counsellor escalation

The system automatically executes the communication layer (Twilio IVR + SMS) for OUTREACH and URGENT tiers. Clinical intervention decisions remain with the human counsellor.

18
How is the distress score explained to the counsellor?

Every alert carries: clinical_reasons list with plain-language strings ("🚨 Explicit self-harm markers detected", "⚖️ Accused Released on Bail", "🎙️ Vocal Tremor / Acute Agitation", "📉 4 consecutive missed check-ins", "⚡ Synergistic Risk Escalation"); weights_used showing actual weights applied; and a Radar Chart in the Victim Detail Modal showing NLP, Speech, Behavioral, and Context sub-scores visually.
